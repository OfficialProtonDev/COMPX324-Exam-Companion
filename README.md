# COMPX324 Exam Companion

An unofficial open-book exam companion for **COMPX324 User Experience Design** (University of Waikato, 2026).

**Open it:** https://officialprotondev.github.io/COMPX324-Exam-Companion/

## What it does
- **39 concept cards.** Every examinable principle, technique and awareness topic from the lecture series. Each card has the lecture's definition, its key points with slide references (e.g. `6-2 p4`), an evaluation checklist, limits and trade-offs, and lecture examples.
- **Instant search** (`/` to focus). Typo-tolerant and ranked, across the cards and any material you add.
- **Build answer.** Type the question in shorthand. The tool detects the concepts and the context (your prototype, your own error, a given scenario), then lays out a Define → Apply → Judge → Improve scaffold for each part, with tickable lecture points. It also produces a copyable outline.
- **Exam plan.** A 2-hour countdown with a review buffer, per-question time budgets weighted by marks, and status tracking (to do / drafting / done / come back).
- **My material.** Drop in the lecture PDFs from Moodle and your own Phase I–III submissions. They're read **in your browser** and stored in IndexedDB on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Keep the original lecture file names (`6-2-COMPX324-2026.pdf`) so the slide references on the cards open the actual slide.

## Notes
- Made by a student, not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Waikato or the COMPX324 teaching team. Lecture slides are **not** included. Bring your own from Moodle.
- Summaries are paraphrased and may contain mistakes. Verify against the slides.
- Check your exam's instructions on which resources and tools are permitted.

Single static file (`index.html`). PDF reading uses pdf.js and Word reading uses mammoth.js, both from cdnjs.
